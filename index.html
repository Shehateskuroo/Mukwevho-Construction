About Mukwevho Construction

Based in Roodepoort, Mukwevho Construction has been helping South African homeowners, developers, and businesses build with confidence. From residential homes and extensions to commercial fit-outs, our qualified, insured team delivers on site safely, on time, and within budget.

We believe every project deserves clear communication, quality materials, and workmanship you can rely on. Your plans are our commitment — from foundation to final handover.
